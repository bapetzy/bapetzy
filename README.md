### About Me

I treat the browser like an architectural drafting table and a real-time lab: typography stays sharp and accessible in the DOM, motion follows physical springs instead of rigid timers, and complex systems earn their place through determinism and verifiable tests.

- **Creative Frontend & 3D Web:** Scroll-choreographed cameras, custom `onBeforeCompile` & standalone GLSL shaders, sunlit matte materials, and non-reactive `useFrame` loops.
- **Simulation & Systems Engineering:** Cellular automata without frame-loop GC churn, discrete-event job queue simulators with independent fault RNG streams, and multi-agent execution harnesses.
- **Engineering Hygiene:** Explicit GPU geometry/material/shadow disposal, `prefers-reduced-motion` locks, keyboard-first navigation, and automated unit + Playwright E2E checks.

### Selected Experiments & Systems

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [**portfolio-agy**](https://github.com/bapetzy/portfolio-agy) | *Atelier Kertas & Cahaya* — Frameless editorial UI paired with S-curve spring camera choreography, floating 3D sculptural islands, and custom paper/plaster GLSL shaders. | `React 19` · `Three.js` · `R3F` · `GLSL` · `Lenis` · `Zustand` |
| [**salt-and-ember**](https://github.com/bapetzy/salt-and-ember) | Closed-jar cellular automata sandbox (`172 x 230`) where 30 materials and 25 weather phenomena emerge from heat, gravity, and raycast light (`~1.2 ms/tick`, 47 unit + 13 E2E tests). | `TypeScript` · `Canvas 2D` · `Vitest` · `Playwright` |
| [**queuecraft**](https://github.com/bapetzy/queuecraft) | Deterministic discrete-event simulator for producers, worker pools, backoff + jitter retries, DLQs, and parallel ghost runs rendered on a mosaic signal-box track panel. | `Svelte 5` · `TypeScript` · `Canvas 2D` · `Vitest` · `Playwright` |
| [**kamar-gelap**](https://github.com/bapetzy/kamar-gelap) | Interactive 35mm darkroom photo essay using scroll time, 2D fluid-wave render targets, vertex-bent hanging prints, and real-time CSS-to-shader lighting transitions. | `Three.js` · `GLSL` · `GSAP` · `Lenis` · `TypeScript` |
| [**atlas-of-unfinished-places**](https://github.com/bapetzy/atlas-of-unfinished-places) | Generative cartography of a fictional estuary across four conflicting survey editions (`1871-1962`), built with seeded height fields and accessible `d3-contour` SVG layers. | `React 19` · `d3-contour` · `SVG` · `TypeScript` · `Vitest` |
| [**registry-of-cast-shade**](https://github.com/bapetzy/registry-of-cast-shade) | Civic design & frontend experiment where every cast shadow on the page is driven by a real solar-position calculation for `52.4 deg N`. | `React` · `Motion` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` |
| [**ai-agent-lab**](https://github.com/bapetzy/ai-agent-lab) | Multi-agent research harness investigating tool permissions, context flow, orchestration, memory, and deterministic evaluation. | `Python` · `Pytest` · `Multi-Agent Systems` |
